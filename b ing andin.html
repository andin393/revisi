PARAGRAPH 1:General Statement Electronic waste has become a growing environmental problem due to rapid technological development. Old computer motherboards, resistors, and broken electronic devices are frequently thrown away when they are no longer usable. However, these discarded electronic parts contain valuable materials and unique textures that can be creatively repurposed. One great way to manage this waste is by transforming old circuit boards and components into a decorative butterfly sculpture. This transformation shows how electronic waste can be turned into a unique and artistic product. poin:he e-waste problem and the creative solution of recycling old circuit boards into an artistic butterfly sculpture..


PARAGRAPH 2: The Transformation Process The creation process begins by collecting unused computer motherboards, small resistors, and wires. The motherboard is carefully cut or shaped into the form of a butterfly’s body to serve as the main structure. Meanwhile, small electronic components like resistors and thin wires are sorted out to be used as legs and antennae. At this stage, the original function of the electronic parts completely changes. Instead of processing data or regulating electricity, they become physical textures and mechanical details for a piece of art. Poin:Gathering materials (computer parts, resistors, wires) and shaping the body, legs, and antennae.


PARAGRAPH 3:The Formation of a New Product The shaped motherboard body is then combined with wings made from patterned translucent materials or cut plastic to represent the butterfly wings. Resistors and wires are attached carefully along the body and head using strong adhesive to look like realistic insect legs and antennae. Different colors and patterns on the wings create a striking contrast against the green circuit board. Once all the components are firmly glued in place and the adhesive dries completely, the various electronic scraps merge into one complete and beautiful decorative butterfly. Poin:Assembling the body with patterned wings, gluing them together, and finishing the piece into a contrasting decoration.


PARAGRAPH 4:The Result and Its Impact As a result, old electronic components are transformed into a stunning butterfly sculpture that has high aesthetic value. This craft can be used as a wall decoration, a display piece, or a unique gift. More importantly, making art from electronic waste helps reduce environmental pollution and promotes creative recycling. Therefore, turning computer scraps into decorative crafts is a simple yet effective way to support a more sustainable environment. Poin:Aesthetic value (decor/gifts) and environmental impact (reducing pollution through creative recycling).
